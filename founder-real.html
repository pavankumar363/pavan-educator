Meet the Founder

Pavan Kumar

Founder of Pavan Educator

Pavan Kumar is the founder of Pavan Educator, an initiative to make quality education simple, practical and accessible for every learner. With a passion for teaching and technology, he aims to help students gain real skills, build projects and prepare for a brighter future.

Through this platform, he shares learning materials, project ideas and video tutorials to support students in their academic and professional journey.
