AI Fundamentals Notes

A clear foundation for understanding artificial intelligence and the technologies built around it.

01

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is the field of creating computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, perception, language understanding and decision-making.

02

Machine Learning

Machine learning allows systems to learn patterns from data instead of being programmed with every rule. Common approaches include supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning.

03

Deep Learning

Deep learning uses multi-layer neural networks to learn complex patterns. It powers many modern applications in language, vision, speech and generative AI.

04

Neural Networks

Neural networks are computational models inspired by connected neurons. They learn by adjusting weights during training so their predictions become more useful.

05

AI Applications

AI is used in recommendation systems, fraud detection, medical image analysis, translation, chatbots, autonomous systems, education and scientific research.

06

Responsible AI

Good AI practice includes checking accuracy, protecting privacy, reducing harmful bias, explaining important decisions and keeping people involved when appropriate.
