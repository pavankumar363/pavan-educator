1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of building computer systems that can perform tasks involving capabilities such as learning, reasoning, perception, language understanding and decision-making.

2. Machine Learning

Machine Learning is a part of AI in which systems learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

3. Deep Learning & Neural Networks

Deep learning uses neural networks with many layers to learn complex patterns. Neural networks are made of connected computational units that transform input information into useful outputs.

4. Real-World Examples

AI is used in recommendations, spam filtering, navigation, translation, image analysis, voice assistants and many other applications.
