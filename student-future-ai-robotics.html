1. AI and Robots

Robotics combines physical machines with software. AI can help robots understand their surroundings, plan actions and adapt to changing conditions.

2. Sensors & Perception

Robots use cameras, distance sensors, touch sensors and other inputs to collect information about the environment.

3. Planning & Control

Planning selects useful actions while control systems turn decisions into safe movement of motors and other hardware.

4. Applications

AI-powered robots are used in manufacturing, logistics, agriculture, healthcare, exploration and research.
