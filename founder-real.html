ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Education with a practical purpose.

Pavan Kumar is the founder of Pavan Educator, an initiative focused on making technical education simple, practical and accessible for learners.

Through Pavan Educator, the goal is to bring courses, study materials, tutorials and real-world projects together so students can move beyond theory and develop useful skills.

The platform is built around a simple idea: learning becomes more meaningful when students can understand a concept, practice it and use it to build something.
