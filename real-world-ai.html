AI in the Real World Notes

See how artificial intelligence is applied to practical problems across industries and everyday life.

01

Healthcare

AI can support medical imaging, research, documentation, risk analysis and personalized care, but high-stakes decisions require professional oversight.

02

Education

AI can provide tutoring support, feedback, content generation, accessibility tools and personalized learning experiences.

03

Agriculture

Computer vision, sensors and predictive models can help monitor crops, identify plant problems, optimize resources and improve farm planning.

04

Business

Organizations use AI for customer support, forecasting, document processing, recommendations, fraud detection and workflow automation.

05

Science and Space

AI helps researchers analyze large datasets, detect patterns, simulate systems and support exploration and scientific discovery.

06

Responsible Deployment

Successful real-world AI requires quality data, security, privacy, evaluation, human oversight and continuous monitoring after deployment.
