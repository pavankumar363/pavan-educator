FUTURE AI • NOTES

Generative AI Notes

Learn the core ideas behind AI systems that generate new content.

01

What is Generative AI?

Generative AI creates new content such as text, images, audio, video and code from learned patterns and user instructions.

02

Large Language Models

Language models learn statistical patterns in text and can generate, summarize, transform and reason over language. Their outputs should still be checked for accuracy.

03

Prompt Engineering

A strong prompt usually gives the task, useful context, constraints and desired output format. Clear prompts make it easier to get consistent results.

04

Multimodal AI

Multimodal systems can work with more than one type of information, such as text plus images, audio or video.

05

Common Uses

Examples include tutoring, brainstorming, coding assistance, document summarization, image creation, content drafting and workflow automation.

06

Limitations

Generative AI can produce incorrect information, biased outputs or invented details. Always verify important facts and protect confidential information.
